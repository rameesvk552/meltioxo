PERFUME MANUFACTURING ERP10 — Business Intelligence Reports Catalog
Module 10
Business Intelligence Reports Catalog
25+ Report Heads covering every aspect of your business — from stock alerts to ROI, product profitability to supplier performance.

📦 Inventory & Stock Report Heads

🚨

Stock Alert Report

All materials below reorder level — SKU, current stock, reorder level, shortage qty, and suggested purchase qty.

📦

Stock Valuation Report

Total inventory valuation — raw materials, packaging, and finished goods valued at weighted average cost.

📊

Stock Movement Report

All stock ins and outs for any date range by material or category. Identify fast vs slow-moving stock.

🔄

Slow & Non-Moving Stock

Materials with zero movement in 30/60/90 days to avoid dead capital tied in warehouse storage.

📅

Expiry Alert Report

Perishable essential oils & chemicals expiring within 30/60/90 days with batch details.

📈

Stock Consumption Trend

Monthly material consumption rates to help forecast upcoming purchase requirements accurately.

🏭 Production & Manufacturing Report Heads

💰

Manufacturing Cost Report

Per-product cost breakdown — raw materials, packaging, and overheads. Know your true production cost per bottle.

📉

Wastage & Loss Report

Production material loss analysis — expected yield vs actual yield per production batch with monetary loss valuation.

📊

Production Output Report

Units manufactured per perfume product by date range — track factory output capacity.

🔍

Batch Traceability Report

Trace any finished perfume bottle batch back to specific raw material batches, suppliers, and purchase dates.
